COURSE

Course title: SAMPLE 101 — Planning a Successful Semester
Term: Fall 2026

MODULE LEARNING OBJECTIVES

Week 1 — Getting Started
By the end of this week, students will be able to:
- Name three priorities for the current semester and explain why each matters
  to them.
- Locate at least two campus support resources relevant to those priorities.

Week 2 — Building a Plan
(not yet written)

Week 3 — Committing to Action
By the end of this week, students will be able to:
- Convert a semester priority into a set of weekly actions with dates.
- Identify one likely barrier to each action and name a response to it.

ASSIGNMENT PROMPTS

Introduction post
Post a short introduction to the class. Name one thing you want this semester
to accomplish and one thing that has gotten in the way of a goal before. Read
two classmates' posts and reply to one of them. Your reply should name
something specific in their post.

Weekly planning reflection
(prompt not yet written)

Semester action plan
Build a plan for one priority you named in Week 1. The plan should list weekly
actions with dates from now through the end of the term, name at least one
barrier you expect and how you will respond to it, and identify one campus
resource you will use. Submit as a single file. Length is up to you; a plan
that fits on two pages is usually a plan you will actually follow.

RUBRIC DIMENSIONS

Introduction post
- Introduction is present and answers both questions asked: 5 points
- Reply names something specific in the classmate's post: 5 points
- Posted on time: 2 points

Semester action plan
- Weekly actions are specific and dated: 10 points
- Barriers are named with a stated response: 10 points
- A campus resource is identified and connected to an action: 5 points
- The plan is legible and internally consistent: 5 points
