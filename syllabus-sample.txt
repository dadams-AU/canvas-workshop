COURSE POLICIES

Late work: Work submitted after the stated deadline receives feedback but may
not receive credit unless the student arranged an extension with the instructor
before the deadline.

Attendance: Students are responsible for reviewing each week's materials and
contacting the instructor when circumstances interfere with their participation.

Academic integrity: Submit work that represents your own thinking. Cite any
sources, tools, or collaborators that contributed to your submission.
